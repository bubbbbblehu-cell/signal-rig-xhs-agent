# SIGNAL RIG — visual language

This document is the aesthetic contract of the agent. Do not treat it as a moodboard that can be freely diluted.

## Core idea

Transform ordinary personal photos into a small digital installation: not a scrapbook, not a social-media collage, not a futuristic dashboard. The result should feel like a fragile physical display system temporarily holding memories and signals.

## Form

- Thin exposed metal / dark graphite structural frame.
- Asymmetrical multi-screen composition with visible negative space.
- Screens sit low or off-center rather than forming a clean grid.
- A small dark-green backing plane may exist, but must remain secondary and smaller than the total structure.
- Photographs remain the protagonist; structural elements organize them rather than decorate them.
- One optional tiny pixel/signal element is allowed as an accent.
- The whole object should plausibly exist as an exhibition installation in a dim gallery.

## Typography

- Sparse, editorial, restrained.
- Small English labels or dates are preferred over large slogans.
- Typography should behave like annotation / archival metadata, not advertising copy.

## Color

- Dark neutral environment.
- Graphite / black / oxidized metal structure.
- Deep muted green can appear as a support color.
- Let the source photos carry most chroma.
- Avoid neon cyberpunk palettes.

## Composition

- Preserve irregularity and tension.
- Avoid equal spacing between every screen.
- Avoid symmetrical 2x2 / 3x3 photo grids.
- Avoid filling the frame with UI panels.
- Allow empty areas; do not fear visual silence.

## Texture and lighting

- Material should read as physical: brushed metal, matte plastic, slightly imperfect monitor surfaces.
- Low-key gallery lighting with subtle reflections.
- No excessive fog, holograms, particles, glowing cables, lens flares or sci-fi spectacle.

## What it is NOT

Never default to:
- Pinterest collage aesthetics
- Y2K sticker boards
- cyberpunk control rooms
- generic Apple-like product render
- maximalist moodboard
- clean dashboard UI
- scrapbook tape / torn-paper collage
- vaporwave or synthwave

## Output feeling

The image should feel personal, quiet, slightly strange, contemporary, and authored — as if someone's recent life has been temporarily wired into a physical memory machine.
